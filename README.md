### Transformation of Explosive Itô Diffusions Using Gamma and Glued Asymptotic Scale Functions

My bachelor thesis in applied mathematics, conducted at the *Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées de l'École Polytechnique* and advised by Maxime Breden and Hugo Chu.

It studies Itô diffusions that explode to infinity in finite expected time, classifies regimes of explosion, and constructs a transform that scales an explosive process into a non-explosive one, for numerical simulation.
